Peer review

Information for Reviewers

Your role

Reviewers advise the editor on whether a manuscript is sound, original and clearly reported, and help authors improve it. The decision belongs to the Editor-in-Chief.

How it works

  1. You receive an email invitation with the title and abstract. Accept or decline from the link within 5 days.
  2. Once you accept, the anonymised manuscript opens in the reviewer portal. It cannot be downloaded.
  3. Complete the structured report within 21 days. You can save a draft and return to it.
  4. You are told the final decision and can read the other reviewers' anonymised comments.

Writing the report

The form asks you to score originality, methodology, analysis, clarity and relevance, give comments to the authors, and give confidential comments to the editor. Then you recommend: accept, minor revision, major revision or reject. Be specific, constructive and courteous. Point to sections, tables or lines where you can.

Confidentiality and conflicts of interest

Manuscripts under review are confidential. Do not share them, use their content, or upload them to generative AI tools. Decline the invitation if you have a conflict of interest, such as a recent collaboration with the authors, a competing project, or a personal or financial interest.

Join the reviewer pool

Create an account in the ACRI Journals portal and tick "I am willing to review", with your subject areas. Editors invite reviewers from this pool.
