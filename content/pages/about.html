About ACRI Journals

ACRI Journals is an open-access journal network and part of ACRIVERSE, based in Ahmedabad, India. It brings ACRI's journals together on one platform with shared standards for peer review, open access, production and preservation, while each journal keeps its own editorial identity, Editor-in-Chief and editorial board.

Mission

We publish research that is rigorous, open and useful to the institutions that shape education, business and public life. Ideas begin with individuals; they last when institutions carry them forward. A well-run journal is one of those institutions.

The journals

The network launches with the International Journal of Business and Management Research Studies (IJBMRS) and the International Journal of Education and Learning Futures (IJELF). New journals join through the ACRI Journals editorial council.

Editorial governance

Each journal is led by one or more Editors-in-Chief, supported by Senior Editors, Editors and an Editorial Board. Every editor is appointed for a fixed term and signs the ACRI Journals Editorial Agreement before starting. Editorial decisions rest with the Editor-in-Chief and are independent of the publisher's commercial or institutional interests. See Editorial independence and governance.

Publishing standards

  • Double-blind peer review by at least two independent reviewers for research articles.
  • Open access on publication under a Creative Commons licence; authors keep copyright.
  • A DOI for every article, minted through Zenodo, with the final PDF archived permanently.
  • Structured, machine-readable metadata on every article for Google Scholar and reference managers.
  • A single typeset standard for every article's web and PDF versions.
